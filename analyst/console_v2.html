HOW IT WORKS

Astrid uses SigNoz to collect metrics about your network traffic — every process, every destination, every byte. When something unusual happens, SigNoz sends an alert to Astrid's AI. The AI explains what's happening in plain English and suggests a fix. You can click a button to apply the fix automatically. Nothing leaves this machine except the question you ask — the AI's only job is to watch, explain, and act when you say so.
